Privacy Policy

Loopback · Last updated: 8 November 2026

Loopback is a single-player game. We built it so that it works without collecting any personal data.

What we collect

Nothing. Loopback does not collect, transmit, sell or share any personal information. There are no accounts, no ads, no analytics and no tracking.

What is stored on your device

Your progress (scores, unlocked items, completed goals and settings) is saved only on your own device. It never leaves your device, and you can delete it at any time under Settings → Reset all progress or by uninstalling the game.

Sharing

If you tap Share, your device's own share menu opens with a short text of your score. Nothing is sent unless you choose an app to share it with, and we never see what you share.

Children

Loopback does not collect data from anyone, including children under 13.

Changes

If this policy ever changes (for example, if we add online features), we will update this page and the date above before the change takes effect.
